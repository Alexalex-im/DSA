Types Of Linked List 

    - Singly Linked List 
    - Circular Singly Linked List 
    - Doubly Linked List 
    - Circular Doubly Linked List 

    Singly Linked List 
        Head | 1 --->  1 | 111 ---> 4 | 333 ---> 5 | null 

        Tail | 333

        Circular Singly Linked List 
         Head | 1 --->  1 | 111 ---> 4 | 333 ---> 5 | null 

        Tail | 333

        Circular Singly Linked List 
         Head | 1 --->  player1 | 111 ---> player2 | 222 ---> player3 | 333 ---> player4 | 001 

        Tail | 333



    4 Player Chess Board 



    Doubly Linked List 

    Head | 001 ---> null | 1 | 111 ---> 001 | 2 | 222 ---> 111 |
     4 |333 ---> 222 | 5 | null 

    Tail | 333

    Ex: 
        Music Player 

    
    Circular Doubly Linked List 

    Head | 001 ---> null | 1 | 111 ---> 001 | 2 | 222 ---> 111 |
     4 |333 ---> 222 | 5 | null 

    Tail | 333  

    Ex:
        cmd + shift + tab
