Time and Space Complexity of Doubly Linked List

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Doubly Linked List               |   Time Complexity      |   Space Complexity   |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Creation                         |      O(1)              |       O(1)           |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Insertion                        |      O(n)              |       O(1)           |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Searching                        |      O(n)              |       O(1)           |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Traversing (forward, backward)   |      O(n)              |       O(1)           |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Deletion of a node               |      O(n)              |       O(1)           |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Deletion of DLL                  |      O(n)              |       O(1)           |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
