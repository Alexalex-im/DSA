Time Complexity of Array vs Linked List 

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                |          Array        |  Linked List        |
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Creation                       |         O(1)          |          O(1)       |
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Insert at first position       |         O(1)          |          O(1)       |
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Insert at last position        |         O(1)          |          O(1)       |
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Insert at nth position         |         O(1)          |          O(n)       |
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Searching in Unsorted data     |         O(n)          |          O(n)       |
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Searching in Sorted data       |         O(log n)      |          O(n)       |
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Traversing                     |         O(n)          |          O(n)       |
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Delete at first position       |         O(1)          |          O(1)       |
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Delete at last position        |         O(1)          |     O(n)/O(1)       |
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Delete at nth position         |         O(1)          |          O(n)       |
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Delete of array/Linked list    |         O(1)          |     O(n)/O(1)       |
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Access nth element             |         O(1)          |          O(n)       |
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
