Time and Space Complexity of Stack using LinkedList

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|    Stack                     |  Time Complexity      |    Space Complexity |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|    Create Stack              |      O(1)             |           O(1)      |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|    Push                      |      O(1)             |           O(1)      |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|    Pop                       |      O(1)             |           O(1)      |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|    Peek                      |      O(1)             |           O(1)      |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|    isEmpty                   |      O(1)             |           O(1)      |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|    Delete Entire Stack       |      O(1)             |           O(1)      |
