Coin Change Problem 

    You are given coins of different denominations and total amount of money. Find the minimum number of coins that you need you make up the given amount.

    Infinite supply of denomination : (1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 1000)

    Example 1

    Total amount : 70 

    Answer : 2 --> 50 + 20 = 70

    Example 2

    Total amount : 122

    Answer : 3 --> 100 + 20 + 2 = 121


    Total amount : 2035

    2035 - 1000 = 1035          Result : 1000, 1000, 20, 10, 5 
    1035 - 1000 = 35
    35 - 20 = 15                Answer : 5
    15 - 10 = 5
    5 - 5 = 0
