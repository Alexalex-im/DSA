BFS vs DFS 

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                    |             BFS                   |           DFS                              |
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| How does it work internally?       |     It goes in breadth first      |  It goes in depth first                    |
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Which DS does it use internally?   |         Queue                     |      Stack                                 |
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Time Complexity                    |         O(V+E)                    |      O(V+E)                                |
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Space Complexity                   |         O(V+E)                    |      O(V+E)                                |
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| When to use?                       |     If we know that the target is |  If we already know that the target vertex |
|                                    |     closed to the starting point  |  is buried very deep                       |
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
