What is Divide and Conquer Algorithm?

    Divide and Conquer is an algorithm design paradigm which works by recursively breaking down a problem into subproblems of similer type, until these become simple enough to be solved directly. The solution to the subproblems are then combined to give a solution to the original problem. 

    Example : Developing a website

            Func 4   Func 4

            

    Property of Divide and Conquer Algorithm 

    Optimal Substructure : 
        If any problem's overall optimal solution can be constructed from the optimal solutions of its subproblem then this problem has optimal substructure.

        Example : Fib(n) = Fib(n-1) + Fib(n-2) 

        Why we need it?

            It is very effective when the problem has optimal substructure property.
